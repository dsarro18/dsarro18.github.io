The Death of Deception

August 13, 2026

Let me preface by saying that those who engage in lying, deception, dishonesty, etc. are morally and ethically shameful. This is in no way an endorsement, nor wishful longing for what I would consider a return to days past. The only purpose is to state an observation from my perspective as a 28-year-old in the year 2026.

People’s skillsets specializing in “stretching or bending” the truth are... poor. I wish there was a better way to articulate this but quite frankly they are all disgraceful. Lackluster. Elementary.

Politicians, Salesmen, Teachers, Corporations all SUCK at creative deception. Notice the differentiation between persuasion and deception. Getting a group of people persuaded has never been easier through the mass media channels available at such a cheap or “free” cost. The potential audience has never been personally so close, always one click away and listening.

But deceptive persuasion? Bending facts to fit a narrative? The slick, quick-witted ability to create doubt in near-certain scenarios? No, those masterminds are nearly extinct. “Killed” by a non-thinking generation which itself was built upon a generation that embraced convenient thinking.

The acceleration towards a brainless society is more noticeable now than ever before in my semi-short lifetime. No, this is not anti-Trump, far from it. He is the result of past generations’ negligence.

The (best oratory) President(s) can (have always been able to) freely state something clearly false and yet people would believe, defend, and act on it without deep questioning. This above is true for the other groups too FYI, teachers, religious leaders, business spokesmen, etc. and is not a recent trend.

What IS a new trend is the degree of nonsensical claims that are defended passionately.

Take Francesca Hong, a Socialist-Democrat who ran for Governor’s Mansion in Wisconsin. She tweeted and stated VERBATIM that she is in favor of overthrowing and abolishing the Senate at the federal level. No code-worded message, just a simple “Abolish the Senate” back in 2021. Aside from the usual hypocrisy from the side of “rules and norms,” the laughable nature of her campaign, the legacy Media ™, and social media Democrat defenders all ADAMANTLY claiming she didn’t say this is astounding. No attempt to spin, no attempt to claim there was a hidden message, missing context, etc. NOTHING! Just simple denial, and the confidence that 50% will look past it without blinking.

This micro-microcosm is easiest to find in politics (simply listen to Roosevelt, Kennedy, or Reagan speak and see the enhanced lexical range with near-perfect diction) but certainly exists everywhere today across every sub-section of everyday life.

Teachers freely teach with an agenda with no attempt to hide it, claim they don’t, then just move on and repeat. Corporations lie to us daily, no apology, then do it again and yet we buy products en masse. No real pushback from either side, just 24 hours of arm flailing and next topic.

Where are the schmoozers? Where are the sleazers? Where are the talkers?

The simplification of deception is hinting at a society so easily convinced not due to improvements in marketing but rather to a freefall of critical thinking.

There is the old “at-home IQ test” that states that those who use sarcasm to argue, for comedic relief, or persuasive defense are inherently more intelligent. Why? They had to understand the premise, understand what the other side was aiming to achieve, and formulate a response that solves their own objective and hints at the absurdity of the other side’s argument near instantaneously. Once you see who in your life uses effective sarcasm you see a different level of intelligence.

The same is true for society embracing primitive tactics for their “lying”. Politicians, education, religion, athletics, science, business ALL reflect the state of society. The worst part of this decline is that so few can see the ripple effect.

Society continues a headfirst dive towards death. And the worst part is no one can ever LIE optimistically to convince me it isn’t happening anymore. They lack the skillset to break through.
