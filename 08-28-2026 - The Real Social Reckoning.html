The "Real" Social Reckoning

August 28, 2026

You can see the future now, and not just in San Francisco.

For decades, there has been a gradual buildup in avoidance. Avoidance of commitment, avoidance of risk, avoidance of accountability, avoidance of acceptance, etc. You get the idea.

The aftereffect on society is immeasurable. Not because you can’t see it unfolding, but the scale is impossible to quantify.

This article should and will be a longer paper analyzing the root causes of what is a non-existent phenomenon in society which is accountability and “real risk.” But alas, today will be a swift attempt to convey initial thoughts.

This week, there were two major news stories that convey the same core idea.

1) META handed a multi-billion-dollar lawsuit settlement in which they will pay restitution to multiple states over the next decade

2) Mark Walter's pending SEC investigation into illegal capital financing and risky investment decisions

The link is evident. Neither of these groups or individuals will truly face consequences.

META pays $1.7 BN a year (less than .1% of their market capitalization rate) just 50% of their daily revenue... which means in 2 days, META has already made $1.7 BN and covered the lawsuit for the year. Oh and the mental health issues they caused by exploiting teens is just a “sorry!”

Mark Walter will sell the Lakers (for profit, roughly $2 BN) and unwind his other ventures. He will probably pay a fine, apologize, get a C-Suite gig somewhere else complete with the Forbes comeback article.

Both scenarios are the norm. Business schools talk about Enron still, which is pitiful. 25 years later, there SHOULD be others that equate to them but failure to enforce and hold those corporations accountable means no new case studies.

Lack of accountability + lack of consequence is a recipe for disaster. Especially when you're using others time, capital, and resources in the first place. Worse, no one even cares enough to act.
